Project 002 · Coming soon

RumahSync

Rumah sewa, selari.

A Malay-language app for shared houses. Rent, water, electricity and household buys are split fairly down to the last sen, and every payment is checked by the house manager.

  • Rent and bills split equally or your own way, to the sen
  • Housemates upload receipts; the manager approves or rejects
  • Monthly reports, CSV export and friendly reminders in Malay
  • Deposits tracked separately from monthly totals
  • Installs on your phone like an app
