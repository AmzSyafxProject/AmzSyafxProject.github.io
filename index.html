Project 001 · Live now · Version 3.1.0

KEMAS

Your PC, Kemas.

A native Windows app that clears junk, sorts your folders and frees memory in one click — with safeguards that never touch what matters.

  • Smart Clean for temp files, caches and the Recycle Bin
  • New: Deep Clean for shader and app caches, crash dumps and Windows leftovers
  • File Organizer with your own folder names and rules
  • Memory Booster and Startup Apps for a faster PC
  • Updates itself, safely, whenever a new version lands
Download for Windows View on GitHub

KEMAS is new, so your browser may say it "isn't commonly downloaded". Choose Keep, then on the blue Windows screen choose More info → Run anyway. Every release is published with a SHA-256 checksum on GitHub.

Project 002 · Coming soon

RumahSync

Rumah sewa, selari.

A Malay-language app for shared houses. Rent, water, electricity and household buys are split fairly down to the last sen, and every payment is checked by the house manager.

  • Rent and bills split equally or your own way, to the sen
  • Housemates upload receipts; the manager approves or rejects
  • Monthly reports, CSV export and friendly reminders in Malay
  • Deposits tracked separately from monthly totals
  • Installs on your phone like an app
