RumahSync

Shared homes, in sync.
BetaEnglishBahasa MelayuLight and dark

A house-management app for shared homes. Rent, water, electricity and household buys are split down to the last sen, and the house owner checks every payment.

  • Sign up as a house owner or a housemate, then invite with a private link
  • Rent and bills split equally or your own way, to the sen
  • Housemates upload receipts; the owner approves or rejects
  • The owner's payment QR appears right where housemates pay
  • Reminders and updates in English or Bahasa Melayu
  • Monthly reports and CSV export, with deposits tracked separately
  • One house is free; more houses come with RumahSync Premium
  • Installs on your phone like an app
