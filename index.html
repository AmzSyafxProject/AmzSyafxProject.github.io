Project 002 · Coming soon

RumahSync

Shared homes, in sync.
EnglishBahasa MelayuLight and dark

A house-management app for shared homes. Rent, water, electricity and household buys are split down to the last sen, and the house manager checks every payment.

  • Rent and bills split equally or your own way, to the sen
  • Housemates upload receipts; the manager approves or rejects
  • The owner's DuitNow QR appears right where housemates pay
  • Reminders and updates in your chosen language, English or Bahasa Melayu
  • Monthly reports and CSV export, with deposits tracked separately
  • Run more than one house from a single account
  • Installs on your phone like an app
