KEMAS Privacy Policy

Effective 17 October 2026 · Applies to KEMAS for Windows, including the Microsoft Store edition.

In short: KEMAS does not collect, store or share any personal information. Everything it does happens on your own PC.

What KEMAS does on your PC

KEMAS scans and removes temporary and cache files, organises files in folders you choose, frees memory and manages startup apps. It reads file names, sizes and dates, and process names, only to do these jobs locally. None of this information leaves your computer.

Information we collect

None. KEMAS has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no telemetry. We never see your files, your settings or how you use the app.

Data stored on your device

KEMAS saves its settings, your organiser rules and undo history in a local folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%\KEMAS). You can open or delete this folder at any time from Settings, and it is removed when you uninstall the app and choose to remove your data.

Internet connections

  • KEMAS may download a short animation file used on the cleaning screen. This is a standard file request; no personal information is sent.
  • The edition downloaded from GitHub checks GitHub Releases for new versions. The Microsoft Store edition does not; Microsoft Store delivers its updates.

Like any web request, these connections reveal your IP address to the server that answers them (the content delivery network or GitHub), under that provider's own privacy policy. We do not receive or keep this information.

Children

KEMAS is a general-purpose utility and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be raised on the KEMAS GitHub page: github.com/AmzSyafxProject/KEMAS/issues.
